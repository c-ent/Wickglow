# Background video licences

Wickglow's scenes are third-party videos, and every one is cleared for use in the app. Rainy Cabin is bundled from `assets/backgrounds/videos/`; the others are served from `public/scenes/v1/` on the website and downloaded on Android when picked. All are encoded to 1080p H.264 with no audio, and each has a thumbnail in `assets/backgrounds/thumbnails/`.

A new scene needs a row here, with its source and licence, before it is added to `VIDEOS` or `PIXEL_ART`.
